THE METHOD THE COMPLETE ROUTINE / 3 MINUTES

THE FIVE-
STEP
clean.

Most people brush and call it done. A real clean happens when every tool works in sequence — floss to disrupt, brush to clean, then a two-step finish on the tongue.

5 Tools
3 Minutes
twice A Day
42 Days To Habit
01 / 30 SEC
— Step 01 / Between the teeth

ANGLED
FLOSS pick.

Start where your toothbrush can't reach. The angled head lets you glide between every tooth — including the back molars that usually get missed. Flossing first clears the way for the brush to actually clean, not just push debris around.

— How to
  • Angle the pick down toward the gumline
  • Slide gently between each tooth
  • Curve around the base of each tooth in a C-shape
  • Use a fresh section for each gap
02 / 90 SEC
— Step 02 / Every surface

BRUSH
YOUR teeth.

Now that the gaps are clear, the brush can do its real job. Hold at a 45-degree angle to the gums. Cover every surface: outer, inner, chewing. Ninety seconds — not the thirty most people give it.

— How to
  • Wet the brush, apply a pea-sized amount of paste
  • Angle at 45° to the gumline
  • Brush in small circles, not back-and-forth
  • Cover outer, inner, and chewing surfaces
  • Don't rinse straight after — let the fluoride work
03 / 30 SEC
— Step 03 / Precision finish

TUFT
detail.

The final pass. The angled tuft brush reaches the tight spots your regular brush can't — behind the back molars, around dental work, along the gumline. A precision finish that leaves nothing behind. The head is small and works hard, so it wears faster than a toothbrush — screw on a fresh one roughly every three weeks.

— How to
  • Target the spots your regular brush misses
  • Small circular motions
  • Focus on gum lines and back teeth
  • Rinse when done
04 / 15 SEC
— Step 04 / Loosen the coating

TONGUE
brush.

Most people ignore the tongue. But most bad breath starts in the mouth — and a coated tongue is its single most common source. The soft bristles work into the small crevices, loosening the coating before you scrape it away.

— How to
  • Stick your tongue out fully
  • Brush from back to front in short strokes
  • Cover the full width, side to side
  • Rinse the brush between passes
05 / 15 SEC
— Step 05 / Lift it all off

TONGUE
scrape.

Everything the brush loosened, the scraper lifts off in one smooth pull. Stainless steel, U-shaped, one motion. This is the step that changes your morning breath.

— How to
  • Place the scraper at the back of the tongue
  • Pull forward slowly with light pressure
  • Rinse the scraper after each pass
  • Repeat 3-5 times until clean
