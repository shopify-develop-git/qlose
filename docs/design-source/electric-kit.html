How the subscription works
Your first box includes the full kit for £59. Every 90 days we send a fresh refill — one brush head, 20 floss heads, four tuft brush heads and a tongue brush — for £18. Ninety days matches standard dental guidance on replacing a brush head. Tuft heads wear faster and are designed to be swapped roughly every three weeks, which is why four are included. Pause, skip, or cancel anytime from your account. No calls, no forms.
Technical specification

  • Charge time: approximately 3 hours
  • Battery: 320 mAh, 3.7 V rated voltage
  • Input: 5 V / 1 A

  • Battery life: around 30 days of twice-daily brushing

Warranty
Your electric toothbrush is covered by a 12-month QLOSE warranty from the date of delivery. This is in addition to your statutory rights under UK consumer law, which are not affected.
Recycling and disposal
This kit contains electrical equipment with a built-in rechargeable battery and must not be disposed of with household waste. WEEE producer registration number to be confirmed before launch.
Returns and hygiene seal
Every kit ships sealed with a belly band and a perforated pull tab. If the seal is intact you can return your kit within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. Once the perforated tab is broken the kit is considered opened and, for hygiene reasons, cannot be returned. This does not affect your statutory rights in respect of faulty goods.
Shipping
Free UK shipping on all subscription orders. One-time orders ship for £3.50 standard or £6.50 express. Orders placed by 2pm ship same day. Delivery in 2-3 working days via Royal Mail tracked.

04 / THE DETAILS

QUESTIONS,
answered.

Is the electric toothbrush waterproof?
Yes — the toothbrush is rated IPX7, meaning it's fully waterproof and can be safely used in the shower. Do not submerge the USB-C charging cable.
How long does the toothbrush battery last?
A full charge lasts around 30 days of twice-daily brushing. Charging via USB-C takes approximately 3 hours from empty to full.
Can I pause or cancel my subscription?
Yes — anytime. Log into your account, and you can pause, skip a delivery, change your frequency, or cancel entirely. No calls, no forms.
Do you ship internationally?
Not yet. We're UK-only for now while we build the brand at home. International shipping is on the roadmap.
Do I need to use all five tools?
Yes — that's the point. Each tool plays a specific role, and the routine only works as a system. Every step is short, and together they add up to a complete clean.
